What am I comparing?

Bare is the complete game with optional effects off. Juiced adds presentation only. Overdrive also changes weapons, enemies and timing. Try switching just one effect while paused. Demo starts a fresh run with automatic movement and aiming so you can concentrate on the effects.

Hunters wear green and shoot slow, telegraphed rounds. Hounds run straight at you. The fox is orange. By default you only move: the fox aims and fires automatically. Choose Move + Space to control when to fire, or Mouse aim for the original controls. Enter starts or restarts; no mouse is needed to play.

The video demonstrates a platform shooter. This version adapts its ideas to an almost top-down arena; it does not reproduce every trick. Death bursts are visual only, and the larger bullets keep their original hitboxes.
